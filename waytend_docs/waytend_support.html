Help with Waytend

Plan your tasks around the time you have. Waytend supports groups, repeating tasks, ordered sequences, and larger tasks divided into daily chunks.

Start with your available hours

Open the menu in Groups and choose Daily schedule to set your daily start and end times. Create a group, then add tasks with realistic durations. You can narrow the available days and times for each group in Edit Group. Nested groups also follow their parents’ available hours.

Waytend may bring upcoming tasks forward when needed to fit them before their due dates. If a task cannot fit, review its warning and adjust your available time, task duration, priority, or deadline as appropriate.

Premium and purchases

Open Premium from the Groups menu to view monthly, yearly, and Lifetime options. Prices shown in the app come from the App Store for your region. Use Restore purchases if you already purchased Premium with the same Apple Account.

If a purchase option is unavailable, check your connection and try again. Pending purchase approval may delay access. For billing or refunds, visit Apple’s purchase support.

Lifetime access overrides subscription access in Waytend, but Apple does not automatically cancel the subscription. Use Manage subscription in the app or your Apple Account settings to stop renewal.

Shared groups

Sharing requires Premium and iCloud. The owner adds each participant’s name, Apple Account email, and role in Edit Group, assigns tasks, and sends that person their private invitation. Open the invitation while signed into the intended Apple Account. Each person needs a different account.

Members see their assigned work; owners and admins can see the group’s work and manage assignments. The Participants button lets admins review individual progress and remove assignments or members.

If an update is waiting, open Waytend on the owner’s device, enter the shared group, and allow it to sync. Then refresh the group on the member’s device. Keep both devices connected to the internet. An owner’s device must sync for changes to reach the group.

If access has been removed, contact the group owner. If an older invitation cannot be opened, ask the owner for a new invitation from the latest version. To leave a group you joined, swipe its row and choose Leave.

Saving or syncing problems

If Waytend reports that work could not be saved, keep the app open, free some device storage if necessary, and use Retry. For iCloud problems, check your internet connection, iCloud sign-in, iCloud storage, and Apple’s system status. Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step: unsynced work may exist only on that device.

Contact support or report misuse

Email dgbgames@outlook.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Waytend version, and a brief description of what happened. A screenshot can help; remove private task details first. Never send your Apple Account password or verification codes.

If someone misuses a shared group, leave the group or ask its owner to remove the participant, and contact us for guidance. We cannot inspect another person’s private iCloud records.
