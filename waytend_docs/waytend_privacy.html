Privacy Policy - Waytend

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

App Name: Waytend
Developer: Blayne Conway

Waytend is a task-planning app provided by Blayne Conway, doing business as DGB Games. Your plans are stored on your devices and, when available, in your Apple iCloud account. We do not operate a separate server that receives your task database.

1. Your plans and iCloud

Waytend stores the groups, task names, durations, priorities, deadlines, schedules, assignments, and completion history you enter or create while using the app. Personal data is saved locally and synced through Apple’s CloudKit service when iCloud is available. Some preferences, such as your daily hours, are stored locally on each device.

Private iCloud records are controlled through your Apple Account. DGB Games cannot browse your private task database through Apple’s developer console. Apple processes iCloud data under its own privacy policy and service terms. Syncing depends on your account, network, available storage, and Apple’s services.

2. When you choose to share

Sharing is optional and requires Waytend Premium. A shared group stores participant names, roles, Apple Account email addresses or account identifiers, task assignments, and completion events. These details support invitations, access control, and collaboration.

The owner and active group admins can see and manage the group’s work and participants. Other members receive the tasks assigned to them and the folders needed to display those tasks. Names of other people assigned to the same work may be visible. Ordinary members do not receive other members’ Apple Account email addresses or account identifiers.

Independent groups keep each member’s completion progress separate. In dependent groups, progress on assigned work is shared with the people who can see that work. Owners and admins can review individual completion history.

Shared updates pass through the owner’s app. Changes, assignment updates, and removal of access take effect as the owner’s device syncs. Syncing may be delayed while the owner is offline or Waytend is not running. Revoking access cannot erase copies or screenshots someone already made.

If you export and send a shared-items file, the recipient receives a separate copy of the exported content. You control where you send it, and later changes or deletion in your app do not recall that file.

3. Purchases

Apple handles payments. Waytend uses StoreKit purchase and subscription status to enable Premium and restore access. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchase verification takes place using Apple’s services; the app does not send receipts to a DGB Games server.

4. Advertising, tracking, and diagnostics

Waytend does not contain advertisements, advertising identifiers, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information or use your plans for advertising.

Apple may provide aggregate App Store statistics and diagnostics according to your Apple settings. If you voluntarily send us a crash report or screenshot, it may contain device details or task text; please remove anything you do not want us to see.

5. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message, and any attachments you send. We use them to respond, investigate problems, and maintain support records for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes or legal obligations. Our email provider processes those messages. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or private information about other people.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete your tasks and groups in Waytend. A shared-group owner can remove participants or delete the group; members can leave. Changes to shared access require a successful owner sync. You can manage iCloud access and storage in your device’s Settings. Removing the app alone may not remove data already in iCloud, backups, or another person’s copy.

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have sent us directly. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object to processing, restrict processing, obtain a copy, or complain to a privacy authority. Because we cannot access your private iCloud task database, changes to that database must be made using your app or Apple Account controls. We can help explain the available steps.

7. Children and policy updates

Waytend is not designed to collect information from children for the developer. If you believe a child has sent us personal information directly, contact us so we can address it. This policy may change as the app changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Blayne Conway at dgbgames@outlook.com.
