1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Bipps (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

By using the app, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or physical address.

However, we may collect limited information automatically, including:

a. Device Information

  • Device or other IDs
  • Approximate Location (IP address)
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • General device settings

b. Advertising Data

  • Ad interactions (e.g., impressions, clicks)
  • Performance data related to ads

c. Identifiers

  • Advertising identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or GAID on Android), only if permitted by the user

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the app
  • Improve gameplay and user experience
  • Display advertisements
  • Monitor and analyze app performance

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

This app uses a third-party advertising service to display ads.

These services may collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Usage data
  • Approximate location

This data is used to provide:

  • Personalized advertisements (where permitted)
  • Non-personalized advertisements

For more information, please review the third party’s privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Tracking and User Consent (iOS)

On iOS devices, we use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework.

  • Users are asked for permission before tracking is enabled
  • If permission is denied, only non-personalized ads are shown

The app may ask users to provide age information to help determine whether to request tracking permission.

We do not independently verify user age.

6. In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (such as removing advertisements).

  • Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store
  • We do not collect or store payment information

7. Children’s Privacy

Our Game is designed for a general audience. We implement a neutral age screen upon first

launch to determine the user's age.

  • Users under 18: We do not knowingly collect personal information. All advertisements served to these users are non-personalized, and we disable the collection of advertising identifiers in compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families Policies. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 18, please contact us at dgbgames@outlook.com for immediate deletion.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

We may share limited data with third-party service providers for the purpose of:

  • Advertising
  • Analytics
  • App functionality

These third parties are responsible for their own data practices.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information collected through the app.

However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

a. For Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and UK (GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you opt-in to personalized ads via the consent coordinates (CMP).
  • Legitimate Interests: To improve our Game, ensure security, and show non-personalized ads.
  • Contractual Necessity: To process In-App Purchases and deliver virtual goods.

Your Rights: You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data; the right to data portability; and the right to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority.

b. For Users in California (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not "sell" your personal information in the traditional sense. However, the use of advertising identifiers to show personalized ads may be considered a "share" or "sale" under California law.

  • Categories of Data Collected: Identifiers (IP address, Ad ID), Geolocation (approximate/city-level), and Internet Activity (app usage).
  • Your Rights: You have the right to Know what data is collected, Delete your data, and Opt-Out of the "sale or sharing" of your personal information.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.
